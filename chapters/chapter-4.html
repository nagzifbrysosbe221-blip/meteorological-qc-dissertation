The monitoring system and changes from the plan

The implemented system preserves records and exposes B0/R/H predictions, reasons and timing. For example, a duplicate result belongs to the additional receipt; the original record is not silently removed. The public explorer filters and exports saved evidence and does not run detection on uploaded observations.

Keep the measures separate

Background cost: H gives 22/8,557 T and 38/8,557 U false-positive value hours; B0/R give zero on the common cohort. These correspond to seven T and eleven U false-alarm segments.

Point recall: for the exact negative 0.5-scale T variant with a 72-hour ramp and 72-hour hold, H has TP 14, FP 16, FN 1,655 and TN 6,872. Recall is 0.84% of eligible affected observations, not a family-wide result.

Delay: temperature H–B0 paired delay is undefined because there are no shared detections. More detections do not by themselves establish earlier paired detection.
