Section 2.1 PDF p. 6

Meteorological data quality and the meaning of a QC flag

QC states distinguish missing, unevaluated and suspicious observations. Weather conditions, exposure and record handling can all affect interpretation. A failed check therefore needs context before it can support a physical-fault diagnosis.

Section 2.2 PDF p. 6

Integrity faults and combined quality control

Combined checks depend on time, neighbouring observations and metadata. More checks need not produce more useful detections. This motivates measuring R–B0 and H–R separately and preserving missing cells, absent slots and duplicate receipts as different conditions.

Section 2.3 PDF p. 6

Sensor drift and reference information

Reference information can reveal a local departure, but the reference and prediction model can also be wrong. Retrospective correction with completed records is different from an alarm issued during replay. The study checks model eligibility and reports unavailable reference-dependent assessments.

Section 2.4 PDF p. 7

Statistical monitoring methods and weather data

EWMA carries information from earlier residuals forward. CUSUM and offline change-point methods address related problems with different settings and information needs. EWMA was selected for an inspectable state and a manageable project scope; those alternatives were not experimentally ranked.

Section 2.5 PDF p. 7

Traceable alerts and practical interpretation

A reviewer needs the check, affected period, supporting values and timing, including why a check did not run. This is the study’s meaning of explainability. Whether intended users understand the explanations was not tested.

Section 2.6 PDF p. 8

Evaluating quality control and alarm performance

A known injection is not certified sensor-health truth. An event hit, point recall, false-positive rate, alarm segment and delay each answer a different question. The study preserves raw point predictions, common eligible denominators and unavailable assessments; one event hit never fills in missed points.
