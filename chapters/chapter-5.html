Section 5.1 PDF p. 19

Answer to the research question

H adds temperature gradual-bias detections under controlled replay, with 121 misses, 22 background false-positive hours and 203 excluded common-background hours. Humidity gains remain exploratory. Additional hits do not establish faster paired detection: temperature H–B0 delay is undefined because there are no common hits.

Section 5.2 PDF p. 19

What the added checks contributed

R adds direct duplicate and timestamp evidence. B0 can receive timestamp-event credit through linked absent slots, while R/H identify timestamps directly five simulated minutes earlier. H adds no integrity-event hits beyond R. H–R combines the prediction model, reference and EWMA, so it does not isolate EWMA alone.

Section 5.3 PDF p. 19

Balancing detection and false alarms

For temperature, alpha 0.005 gives 58 hits and 10 background false positives; primary gives 95 and 22; alpha 0.02 gives 121 and 56. These use 216 events and 8,557 eligible background hours. The replay does not establish investigation costs or an acceptable alarm burden, so no post-result optimum follows.

Section 5.4 PDF p. 19

Relationship to literature

The findings support evaluating added components instead of assuming that combining checks guarantees improvement. They also support retaining raw point predictions beside event credit and treating reference information as uncertain. Differences between datasets and fault designs prevent a numerical ranking against the cited studies.

Section 5.5 PDF p. 20

What the study cannot establish

One target station-year, synthetic isolated faults, shared weather, uncertain background/reference truth and earlier data exposure restrict generalisation. Humidity needs an eligible model and fresh validation. Independent reproduction, intended-user studies and prospective operational evaluation remain future work.

Section 5.6 PDF p. 21

Progress against objectives and future work

The five objectives produced a literature synthesis, documented design, implemented artefact, controlled comparison and critical analysis. Their assessment is qualified: records support traceability, but operational alarm acceptance, physical diagnosis, useful deployment and participant usability remain unproven.
