Section 1.1 PDF p. 4

Problem statement and business case

Fixed ranges can miss a gradually developing bias while a reading still looks plausible. Additional monitoring may help, but can also add alarms or be unavailable. The intended benefit is evidence for prioritising investigation; savings and reduced workload were not measured.

Section 1.2 PDF p. 4

Research question aim and objectives

The question asks how effectively hybrid QC detects sensor drift and data integrity faults compared with a threshold-only baseline, while maintaining a low false alarm rate. Here, drift is represented by controlled gradual-bias injections. “Low” is described through measured rates and burden; an operational acceptance threshold was not established.

Section 1.3 PDF p. 5

Project scope and system overview

The study monitors historical KNMI temperature (T) and relative humidity (U). B0 supplies missing-data and range checks; R adds duplicate and timestamp checks; H adds a prediction model, reference information and residual EWMA. Humidity remains exploratory. The website displays saved results.
