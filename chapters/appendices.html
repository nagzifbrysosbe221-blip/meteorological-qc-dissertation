A quick reference

Key terms, in plain language

QC flag+

A check result identifying a suspected condition for investigation, not a confirmed physical defect.

Residual+

The observed value minus its model prediction. The monitor standardises this using frozen residual statistics.

EWMA+

An exponentially weighted moving average: a recursive state that carries earlier residual evidence forward.

Root / event hit+

A root groups an injected change and its consequences as one event. Compatible evidence can give that event one hit within its matching window.

Point recall+

The proportion of eligible affected observations flagged: TP / (TP + FN). An event hit does not turn other missed points into detections.

Common eligible cohort+

The same labelled units with every required check complete across the compared configurations. Point scores are conditional on this coverage.

Coverage+

How much of the scheduled or affected record could be assessed. Completed execution does not mean every check was available.

Assumed normal+

Background observations treated as normal for scoring, with recorded uncertainty. Their sensor health is not certified.

False-positive rate+

False-positive points divided by eligible negative points: FP / (FP + TN). It is distinct from the number of alarm segments.

Exploratory humidity+

Humidity continued with an unchanged model despite failed original eligibility and negative validation. Later hits do not remove these limitations.

Null+

Undefined, unavailable or inapplicable according to the field. It does not mean zero.

Paired delay+

A delay comparison for events detected by both configurations. Without shared detections, the contrast is undefined.
