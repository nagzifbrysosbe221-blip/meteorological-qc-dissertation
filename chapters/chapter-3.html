Section 3.1 PDF p. 10

Research approach and rationale

The artefact was constructed and refined through bounded correctness checks. Frozen comparative replay then evaluated known interventions in an artificial setting. This supports an inspectable experiment without establishing natural-fault reproduction or hybrid superiority.

Section 3.5 PDF p. 12

Comparative replay and fault scenarios

Each case applies one prescribed fault recipe and has an unchanged counterpart with the same weather and prior context. Seven families create 936 cases per pass across 12 months. Continuous uninjected replay supplies primary background exposure; reused counterparts do not multiply that exposure.

Section 3.6 PDF p. 13

Evaluation measures and analysis

Point-level comparisons use the same eligible labelled units with all required checks complete. An event earns at most one hit from compatible evidence within its window, among effective roots with valid paired runs. Point recall is the proportion of eligible affected observations flagged. Unavailable points are not true negatives; undefined ratios and miss delays remain null.

Section 3.7 PDF p. 14

Ethics reproducibility and validity

No human participants were involved. Inputs, negative results, failures and retries are preserved. Saved-record reconciliation checks consistency; it does not establish complete raw-data-to-result reproduction. Synthetic faults, uncertain background truth, corrected archives and one target station-year limit inference.
