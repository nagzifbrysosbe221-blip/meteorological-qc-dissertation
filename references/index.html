KNMI data source and interpretation

The study used KNMI's hourly climatology download route for stations 240 (Schiphol) and 260 (De Bilt), requesting fields T and U. T is supplied in tenths of a degree Celsius; U is relative humidity in percent. RH denotes precipitation, not relative humidity. The source route did not supply a separate observation-quality flag for these fields.

The recorded source review on 7 October 2026 identified KNMI's default CC0 terms unless an exception applies. See KNMI's copyright information and scripted download guidance. This route is distinct from the separately cited KNMI Data Platform product. KNMI did not participate in or necessarily endorse this derived work.

The source archive may already contain KNMI corrections or filling. The study did not add filling of missing observations. Temperature conversion, source-period assignment and artificial-fault construction are study transformations; they are not new KNMI observations.
