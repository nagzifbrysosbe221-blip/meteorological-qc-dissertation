Period 4 of 4 / Completed final comparison

2024 — Final evaluation

Read, copy or download the complete Python code ↓

The final period compared three monitoring configurations on the same controlled faults and assumed-normal background. The models and settings established earlier were retained, including humidity’s exploratory status and negative validation finding.

Principal result. In the primary temperature gradual-bias comparison, hybrid monitoring H detected 95 of 216 cases, while B0 and R detected none. H still had 121 evaluated misses and raised 22 false-positive value hours among 8,557 eligible background negatives. The result shows a gain within these cases, accompanied by misses and false alarms.

All 3,748 jobs completed: 3,744 constructed fault cases and four uninjected backgrounds. The saved processing completed in October 2026. The final period is not described as a newly untouched holdout; prior exposure remains disclosed.

1 / Practical sequence

How the final evaluation was carried out

  1. Prepared the final observations and labels. The restricted intake preserved original data and checked their structure. Background-label review was kept separate from detector outputs. It did not establish that the sensors were healthy, and it was not independent human review.
  2. Recorded the final scientific freeze. The accepted data, implementation, prediction models, monitoring settings and scoring rules were bound to saved file identities before the final batch. Humidity’s failed original eligibility and negative 2023 finding remained part of those records.
  3. Completed a measured pilot and the prescribed batch. Each pass included one continuous background and 936 fault cases. Checkpoints preserved completed work and the history of stopped, failed or abandoned attempts.
  4. Replayed each fault and its unchanged counterpart. The three configurations received the same input sequence within each copy. They processed current observations in time order. The detector did not receive the hidden fault labels or original values that could reveal how to repair the input.
  5. Joined the evaluation labels after prediction. Saved flags, availability states and alarm episodes were assessed against known injected faults and assumed-normal labels. Misses and unavailable checks remained visible.
  6. Combined the saved results. A separate analysis produced twelve complete datasets and 52 figures. It read saved outputs without running the detector again. This page presents selected results from those completed tables.

Sources: final freeze, saved design, analysis scope and completion evidence. Dissertation: Sections 4.3–4.4 and Appendix E2–E3.

What B0, R and H actually did

ConfigurationChecks includedPurpose in the comparison
B0 — Basic checksExpected-row availability, explicit missing-cell checks, and fixed value ranges.Establish what the simpler checks could detect.
R — Added integrity rulesAll B0 checks, plus invalid timestamps, off-grid timestamps and duplicate receipts.Measure the contribution of direct record-integrity checks.
H — Hybrid monitoringAll R checks, plus the running monitor of prediction errors using the selected P model and reference observations.Measure the additional contribution of residual monitoring.

Unusable or non-finite inputs could leave a check unavailable; they were not silently treated as healthy readings. The primary comparison was H against B0. R against B0 and H against R helped explain where differences came from. H against R assesses the whole residual-monitoring component, including its prediction model and reference data; it does not isolate the EWMA calculation alone.

Both statistical weights remained 0.20. Primary thresholds remained 2.4816976238219928 for temperature and 2.057779719375828 for humidity. Primary value ranges were −40 to 50 °C and 0 to 100% relative humidity. The four prescribed passes changed only the settings described in the sensitivity section.

Which 2024 observations were scored?

Final scoring covered original source dates 2 January–31 December 2024: 365 days and 8,760 expected hourly slots per station. Although 2024 was a leap year, 1 January was excluded from scoring and supplied 24 hours of starting context.

Continuous background, monthly cases and starting context

Each pass replayed the full background continuously, without resetting at month boundaries. Fault cases and their counterparts used full source-month windows. January had one earlier context day; February–December had fourteen earlier context days, or 336 hours, before their scored month.

Context initialised the monitor and was not scored. A one-hour completion period after each window allowed outstanding processing to finish without adding scored observations. Source dates still owned their records after hour 24 was converted to the next UTC midnight.

The continuous background began with only 24 context hours. The statistical monitor required 228 valid updates before it was ready. This left the first 203 scored hours outside H’s eligible value-hour count. The same excluded hours were removed from the shared comparison with B0 and R.

Sources: fixed settings, context and scored windows, shared replay implementation. Dissertation: Sections 3.4–3.6 and Appendix A4/C.

Exact sizes and durations

Missing-cell and absent-row cases lasted 1, 6, 7 or 24 hours. Duplicate, invalid-time and off-grid cases lasted 1, 6 or 7 hours, crossed with their two specified alternatives.

Completion describes construction. All four passes completed all 936 cases with full construction support and valid pairs: 3,744 effective cases in total. In each pass, all 79,896 intended units were changed, with none unsupported or supported-but-unchanged. This confirms that the faults were constructed; it does not mean that they were all detected.

4 / Meaning of the result measures

What was counted, and why the denominators differ

Read evaluation in four steps. First check whether a fault case was constructed and executed. Then check whether the relevant method could evaluate it. Next inspect hits, misses and, where comparable, detection delays. Finally compare these gains with the separately measured background false alarms and coverage. Successful construction is not a successful detection.

MeasureWhat it asksHow to read it
Fault-case detectionWas the injected fault detected by a compatible check within its declared interval?Detected root events ÷ valid paired cases. A “root” is the original injected fault, including any permitted linked consequence.
Individual-reading resultsWhich eligible affected and unaffected readings were flagged?Point counts retain detected faulty readings, missed faulty readings, false positives and correct negatives. Detecting one case does not mean detecting every affected reading.
Background false alarmsHow much did each configuration flag when no faults were injected?False-positive units and false-alarm segments on the separate continuous assumed-normal background.
Detection delayFor faults both configurations detected, which flagged earlier?Compare delay only on common detections. Misses keep an undefined delay and their observation horizon; they are not assigned zero delay.

Value hours, availability slots and timestamp records were separate scoring tasks. They were not pooled into one denominator. The primary detection table below uses annual, full-support case groups.

Misses were classified. An evaluated miss means a compatible check had an opportunity but did not detect the fault. An unavailable miss means no compatible opportunity was available. A capability miss means the configuration lacked the required check. B0’s duplicate-receipt misses belong to the last category.

The final root-hit rule did not require the counterpart to be unflagged at the same hour. A separate stricter paired-response diagnostic tested that question. This is one reason the 2022 selection counts and final 2024 detection counts should not be treated as identical measures.

Sources: scoring and aggregation contract and root outcome table. Dissertation: Appendices C and E5–E7.

Delay, stricter paired responses and recovery

H and B0 shared no temperature gradual-bias detections, so their paired delay difference was undefined. For humidity, both detected 94 cases, H alone detected 60, and neither detected 62. Post-intervention recovery and remaining alarm activity were recorded separately. A later flag outside the declared fault interval could not turn an earlier miss into a detection. Saved replay delays are not measurements of live operational response time.

6 / False alarms and assessment coverage

The cost alongside the detection gain

For a fair value comparison, B0, R and H were assessed on the same 8,557 eligible hours out of 8,760 (97.68%) for each variable. The other 203 hours were excluded from this shared assessment. B0 and R could assess all 8,760 hours on their own; H’s starting readiness limited the shared count.

A segment groups consecutive eligible false-positive units under the scoring rules. The 22 temperature flagged hours formed seven segments; they were not 22 separate incidents. The normalised segment rate allows the burden to be stated against a fixed amount of eligible exposure.

Only the full continuous background supplied this exposure. Reusing unchanged counterparts across fault cases did not create additional independent background hours. The labels were assumed normal, not a certification of sensor health, and no acceptable operational alarm budget was established.

Unavailable checks and undefined ratios

Complete construction support did not mean every check could evaluate every affected hour. In the primary gradual-bias cases, H had all required checks available at 37,020 of 38,016 affected opportunities per variable. Every case still had at least one compatible opportunity, so its remaining root misses were evaluated misses.

Background recall was undefined because the background had no labelled positive faults. B0/R background precision was undefined because they made no positive predictions. These are missing denominators, not zero performance scores or evidence of perfect sensor health.

The 2023 validation rates used the statistical monitor alone on 8,760 ready hours. These 2024 figures use the final combined configuration and shared eligible value-hour denominator. They should not be compared as if scope and exposure were identical.

Sources: background point counts and segment burden. Dissertation: Section 4.4–4.5 and Appendix E4.

8 / Interpretation

What the final findings establish

  • Residual monitoring added gradual-bias detections in this study. Temperature provides the originally eligible comparison. Its 121 primary H misses and added background flags remain part of the result.
  • Integrity checks provided capabilities that B0 lacked. Direct duplicate and timestamp checks made some faults identifiable earlier and with more specific explanations, even when root totals were equal.
  • Humidity remains exploratory. Its stronger detection total does not reverse the 2022 model failure or negative 2023 validation.
  • Transfer beyond the study is limited. One target station, a reference whose condition is not independently certified, controlled fault shapes, seasonal dependence and assumed-normal background constrain the conclusions.
  • The evidence supports comparison and traceability. It does not demonstrate physical sensor diagnosis, acceptable operator workload, field readiness or participant-tested usability. No overall cross-family ranking or newly untouched holdout claim is made.

How the four periods connect. The 2021 data established the prediction models; 2022 selected models and alarm settings; 2023 exposed a transfer limitation; and 2024 measured the fixed comparison while preserving that limitation. The final account includes detections, misses, unavailable checks, false alarms and adverse sensitivity changes.

def write_lines(path, rows):
    with path.open('x',encoding='utf-8',newline='\n') as stream:
        for row in rows: stream.write(json.dumps(row,allow_nan=False)+'\n')


class DiskSink:
    def __init__(self, folder, name, stack):
        self.folder,self.name,self.stack = folder,name,stack
        self.files={}; self.count=0; self.last_progress=None
    def append(self, row):
        stamp = row.get('slot_utc') or row.get('received_at') or row['emitted_at']
        dt = utc(stamp)
        if self.name=='ledger' and row['check'] not in ('Q04','Q05','Q06'): dt-=timedelta(minutes=5)
        month=(dt-timedelta(hours=1)).strftime('%Y-%m')
        if month not in self.files:
            folder=self.folder/month;folder.mkdir(exist_ok=True)
            f=self.stack.enter_context((folder/(self.name+'.jsonl')).open('x',encoding='utf-8',newline='\n'))
            writer=None
            if self.name=='ledger':
                c=self.stack.enter_context((folder/'ledger.csv').open('x',encoding='utf-8',newline=''))
                writer=csv.writer(c);writer.writerow(row.keys())
            self.files[month]=(f,writer)
        f,writer=self.files[month]
        f.write(json.dumps(row,allow_nan=False)+'\n')
        if writer: writer.writerow(json.dumps(v,allow_nan=False) for v in row.values())
        self.count+=1
        if self.name=='states' and row['variable']=='U':
            day=stamp[:10]
            if day!=self.last_progress:
                print('REPLAY_DAY '+day,flush=True);self.last_progress=day


def monitor_save(folder, public, slots, settings, bounds, provenance, run_id):
    folder.mkdir()
    save_json(folder/'public-inputs.json',public);save_json(folder/'schedule.json',slots)
    save_json(folder/'provenance.json',provenance)
    with ExitStack() as stack:
        sinks={k:DiskSink(folder,k,stack) for k in STREAMS}
        run=_replay([Receipt.from_public(r) for r in public],slots,settings,run_id=run_id,
            protocol='submitted-v2-final-scientific-v1',purpose=provenance['purpose'],bounds=bounds,sinks=sinks)
        meta={k:v for k,v in run.items() if k not in STREAMS}
        meta['stream_counts']={k:s.count for k,s in sinks.items()}
    save_json(folder/'run.json',meta)
    save_json(folder/'completed.json',{'state':'monitor_complete','files':inventory_files(folder)})


def verify_monitor(folder):
    done=read(folder/'completed.json')
    if done.get('state')!='monitor_complete': raise ValueError('Monitor did not complete')
    verify_files(folder,done['files'])
    actual=inventory_files(folder);actual.pop('completed.json')
    if actual!=done['files']: raise ValueError('Unlisted monitor evidence')
    run=read(folder/'run.json');slots=read(folder/'schedule.json')
    if run['slots_closed']!=len(slots) or run['drain_until']!=(utc(slots[-1])+timedelta(hours=1)).isoformat():
        raise ValueError('Monitor truncated its schedule/drain')
    for name in STREAMS:
        count=sum(1 for p in folder.glob('*/'+name+'.jsonl') for _ in lines(p))
        if count!=run['stream_counts'][name]: raise ValueError('Missing saved stream rows')
    return run,read(folder/'public-inputs.json'),slots


def read_units(folder, case_id, month=None):
    meta=read(folder/'run.json'); public=read(folder/'public-inputs.json');slots=read(folder/'schedule.json')
    paths=sorted(p for p in folder.iterdir() if p.is_dir() and (month is None or p.name==month))
    run={**meta,**{name:[r for p in paths for r in lines(p/(name+'.jsonl'))] for name in STREAMS}}
    if month is not None:
        slots=[s for s in slots if (utc(s)-timedelta(hours=1)).strftime('%Y-%m')==month]
        public=[r for r in public if (utc(r['received_at'])-timedelta(hours=1)).strftime('%Y-%m')==month]
        run.update(slots_closed=len(slots),drain_until=(utc(slots[-1])+timedelta(hours=1)).isoformat())
    return adapt(run,public,slots,case_id=case_id),public,slots


def background_generation(originals, case_id):
    root=planned_root({'case_id':case_id,'family':'missing_cell','variable':None,
        'planned_onset':originals[0]['timestamp'],'length_hours':1,'variant':'continuous_background'})
    root.update(construction='no_effect',C=0,Z=1,U=0,accounting_assessed=True,unassessed_units=0)
    public=[public_copy(r) for r in originals]
    return {'public':public,'counterpart':public,'root':root,
            'lineage':[{'identity':r['identity'],'parent_id':r['identity'],'original':r} for r in originals]}


def compact_nodes(units):
    return [{k:v for k,v in n.items() if k!='checks'} for n in raw_nodes(units)]


def source_months(nodes, truth):
    result={}
    for n in nodes:
        key=n['key'] if n['domain']!='timestamp' else unit_key(n['case_id'],'Q04',n['subject'])
        result[n['key']]=truth[key]['source_month']
    return result


def score_background(public_dir, private_dir, generated, win):
    case_id=generated['root']['case_id']; folder=public_dir/'background'
    verify_monitor(folder)
    months=sorted(p.name for p in folder.iterdir() if p.is_dir())
    all_nodes=[]
    # First pass is truth-free. No labels are opened until full raw episodes exist.
    for month in months:
        units,_,_=read_units(folder,case_id,month)
        all_nodes.extend(compact_nodes(units));del units;gc.collect()
    episodes=raw_episodes(all_nodes)
    save_json(public_dir/'raw-episodes.json',episodes)
    from final_labels import load_labels, reviewed_truth
    labels=load_labels(REVIEW/'numeric-labels.jsonl',REVIEW_HASH)
    allowed=defaultdict(set); ownership={}; totals={}; scored_keys=set()
    for month in months:
        print('SCORE_MONTH '+month,flush=True)
        units,receipts,slots=read_units(folder,case_id,month)
        part={**generated,'public':receipts,'counterpart':receipts}
        truth=reviewed_truth(part,{**win,'slots':slots},labels,background=True)
        write_lines(private_dir/(month+'-truth.jsonl'),({'key':k,**v} for k,v in truth['units'].items()))
        points=point_scores(units,truth['units']);save_json(public_dir/(month+'-points.json'),points)
        for p in points:
            key='|'.join((p['task'],str(p['variable']),p['configuration']))
            t=totals.setdefault(key,{cohort:{k:0 for k in ('TP','FP','FN','TN','n')} for cohort in ('own','common')})
            for cohort in t:
                for k in t[cohort]: t[cohort][k]+=p[cohort][k]
        nodes=compact_nodes(units);ownership.update(source_months(nodes,truth['units']))
        for n in nodes:
            k=n['key'] if n['domain']!='timestamp' else unit_key(n['case_id'],'Q04',n['subject'])
            if truth['units'][k]['scope']=='scored':scored_keys.add(n['key'])
        for cfg in CONFIGS:
            for cohort in ('own','common'):
                allowed[cfg,cohort].update(normal_ids(units,truth['units'],cfg,cohort))
        del units,truth,nodes,points;gc.collect()
    groups=defaultdict(list)
    for n in all_nodes:groups[(n['case_id'],n['configuration'],n['domain'],n['variable'])].append(n)
    burdens=[]
    for group,nodes in sorted(groups.items(),key=str):
        keys={n['key'] for n in nodes};eps=[e for e in episodes if tuple(e['group'])==group]
        for cohort in ('own','common'):
            burdens.append({'group':list(group),'cohort':cohort,
                **project_burden(nodes,eps,allowed[group[1],cohort]&keys,ownership),
                'raw_replay_counts_include_unscored_context':True,
                'raw_positive_units_scored':sum(n['prediction'] is True and n['key'] in scored_keys for n in nodes),
                'raw_episodes_with_scored_positive':sum(any(n['key'] in scored_keys for n in e['nodes']) for e in eps)})
    for t in totals.values():
        for counts in t.values():
            d=counts['FP']+counts['TN'];counts['fpr']={'value':counts['FP']/d if d else None,
                'denominator':d,'reason':None if d else 'no_truth_negatives'}
    save_json(public_dir/'background-evaluation.json',{'annual_counts':totals,'burden':burdens,
        'labels':LABELS,'monthly_point_files':[m+'-points.json' for m in months],
        'primary_exposure_source':'this continuous run only; no monthly resets',

def score_pair(public_dir, private_dir, generated, win):
    case_id=generated['root']['case_id']
    for side in ('injected','counterpart'): verify_monitor(public_dir/side)
    units,receipts,slots=read_units(public_dir/'injected',case_id)
    controls,control_public,_=read_units(public_dir/'counterpart',case_id+'-control')
    episodes=raw_episodes(compact_nodes(units));save_json(public_dir/'raw-episodes.json',episodes)
    save_json(public_dir/'counterpart-raw-episodes.json',raw_episodes(compact_nodes(controls)))
    from final_labels import load_labels, reviewed_truth
    labels=load_labels(REVIEW/'numeric-labels.jsonl',REVIEW_HASH)
    truth=reviewed_truth(generated,win,labels)
    control_truth=reviewed_truth(generated,win,labels,counterpart=True)
    save_json(private_dir/'truth.json',truth);save_json(private_dir/'counterpart-truth.json',control_truth)
    health={(case_id,c):{'valid':True,'reason':None} for c in CONFIGS}
    diagnostics={}
    settings_id=identity(read(public_dir/'injected/run.json')['settings'])
    control_settings_id=identity(read(public_dir/'counterpart/run.json')['settings'])
    for cfg in CONFIGS:
        select=lambda rows:[u for u in rows if u['task']=='value' and u['configuration']==cfg]
        changed={(s,generated['root']['variable']) for s in generated['root']['actual_change_mask']}
        diagnostics[cfg]=(excess_response(select(units),select(controls),changed,settings_id,control_settings_id)
            if generated['root']['family'] in ('gradual_bias','out_of_range') else {'status':'inapplicable'})
    save_json(public_dir/'evaluation.json',{'point_scores':point_scores(units,truth['units']),
        'counterpart_point_scores':point_scores(controls,control_truth['units']),
        'root_outcomes':root_scores(truth['roots'],units,health),'excess_response':diagnostics,
        'labels':LABELS,'primary_normal_exposure_hours':0})
    for side,items in (('injected',units),('counterpart',controls)):
        write_lines(public_dir/(side+'-recovery.jsonl'),recovery_units(items,generated['root']['planned_onset'],
            generated['root']['length_hours'],win['last_scored']))


def execute(job, public_dir, private_dir, freeze_folder):
    start=time.perf_counter();freeze=verify_freeze(freeze_folder)
    pass_id=job['pass'];settings,bounds,proof=load_frozen(pass_id)
    frozen=read(freeze_folder/'settings.json')[pass_id]
    if {v:asdict(s) for v,s in settings.items()} != {v:{**s,'coefficients':tuple(s['coefficients'])} for v,s in frozen['settings'].items()}:
        raise ValueError('Settings changed after freeze')
    case=job.get('case');win=window(None if case is None else int(case['source_month'][-2:]))
    if case is not None and case not in roster(): raise ValueError('Unspecified final case')
    from final_data import load_originals
    originals=load_originals(freeze_folder,win)
    scales={'kind':'frozen_scientific',**{v:s.scale for v,s in settings.items()},
            'model_sha256':{v:s.model_sha256 for v,s in settings.items()}}
    g=background_generation(originals,job['job_id']) if case is None else _build(case,originals,scales,'frozen_scientific')
    save_json(private_dir/'construction.json',{**g,'public':None,'counterpart':None})
    save_json(public_dir/'window.json',win);save_json(public_dir/'job.json',job)
    provenance={'purpose':'final performance; assumed-normal background; exploratory humidity R1',
        'freeze_sha256':sha256(freeze_folder/'freeze.json'),'labels':LABELS,'scientific_adapter':proof,
        'actual_scientific_freeze':True,'pass':pass_id,'input_identity':identity(g['public']),
        'counterpart_identity':identity(g['counterpart'])}
    save_json(public_dir/'provenance.json',provenance)
    status=g['root']['construction']
    if g['public'] is not None:
        for side,key in ([('background','public')] if case is None else [('injected','public'),('counterpart','counterpart')]):
            print('MONITOR '+side+' '+job['job_id'],flush=True)
            monitor_save(public_dir/side,g[key],win['slots'],settings,bounds,provenance,job['job_id']+'-'+side)
        print('MONITORS_SAVED; VERIFY_AND_SCORE '+job['job_id'],flush=True)
        if case is None:score_background(public_dir,private_dir,g,win)
        else:score_pair(public_dir,private_dir,g,win)
    else:
        save_json(public_dir/'construction-disposition.json',g['root'])
    verify_freeze(freeze_folder)
    save_json(public_dir/'worker-completed.json',{'state':'complete','job_id':job['job_id'],'pass':pass_id,
        'kind':job['kind'],'construction':status,'labels':LABELS,
        'configurations':list(CONFIGS),'freeze_sha256':sha256(freeze_folder/'freeze.json'),
        'public_files':inventory_files(public_dir),'private_files':inventory_files(private_dir),
        'primary_normal_exposure_hours':win['scored_hours'] if case is None else 0,
        'exposure_count_meaning':'scheduled background hours, not eligible performance denominator',
        'worker_seconds':time.perf_counter()-start,'peak_process_bytes':process_peak_bytes(),'finished_utc':utc_now()})
